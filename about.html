The person doing the cooking

A bit about me

Hi, I'm Venkatesh 👋

I build apps for myself first — small things that fix a problem I actually have — and then I hand them to anyone else with the same problem. Right now that's MoneyBook, a personal finance app I'm getting ready to launch.

I care about clean interfaces, software that opens instantly, and finishing what I start instead of letting it rot after v1. Everything I make ends up here, free, for as long as I'm around to maintain it.

You don't measure a home-cooked meal by the price per plate. You measure it by the faces of the people eating it.

Why I give it all away

The short version of the thing this whole site is built on.

Creation isn't a transaction

Every tool now comes with a subscription; every craft comes with a meter. That's a choice, not a law — and it isn't the one I want to make.

Hospitality is the better impulse

Cooking for people you like is one of the few things nobody does for money. Software at its best should feel the same — a feast prepared for friends.

The reaction is the payment

The joy of making something lies in the reaction of the guest, not the size of the tip. A message saying "this helped" is worth more to me than a sale.

AI is my sous-chef

How these apps actually get made — said plainly, because you deserve to know.

I think of AI as my sous-chef. It helps me chop the vegetables and prep the kitchen, but the recipe, the flavour, and the final dish are entirely mine.

I'm not here to pretend I wrote every single line of code from scratch. Modern AI tools handle a lot of the heavy lifting behind the scenes. My real contribution is the intent behind the build. I sit down and ask myself: Why do I actually want this? How should it feel when I use it? What would make this a joy to open every day?

I direct the design, tweak the feel, and shape the experience until it's something I genuinely love using. AI helps me turn those thoughts into working apps, but the heart and direction come straight from me.

Guided by taste

AI handles the raw code, but I shape the experience. Every button, layout, and feature exists because I wanted it to feel just right.

Built for real life

I only make things I actually need or want for myself, which keeps every app simple, practical, and honest.

Always free

Just like offering a home-cooked meal to a friend, I don't believe in charging for things made out of passion. You're welcome to it all.
