Android signing certificate
---------------------------
Every MoneyBook APK is signed with the same key. Android checks this itself and
will refuse an update signed by anyone else, so this fingerprint is also how you
know a future version really came from here. It is unchanged since 1.4.1, which
is why 1.4.2 installs cleanly over it.
