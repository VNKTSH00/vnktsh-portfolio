Privacy Policy

MoneyBook for iPhone runs in your browser and keeps your entries in your own account, so they're there whenever you sign in. Here's exactly what that means.

Effective 30 September 2026 · Covers the web app at app.vnktsh.com. The Android, Mac and Windows apps collect nothing — their policy is here.

01 What's stored

Your email address and password, to sign you in. The password is stored only as a one-way hash — nobody, including me, can read it.

What you put into the app: entries, accounts, categories, budgets, scheduled entries, your settings, and any receipt photos you attach.

That's all. No location, no contacts, no device identifiers, no browsing history.

02 Where it's kept, and who can see it

Your data is stored with Supabase, a database provider, on servers in Mumbai, India. It travels encrypted (HTTPS) and is encrypted at rest.

Every row is locked to your account at the database level: another user signed in to MoneyBook cannot read or change anything of yours, whatever they try.

As the person who runs the service, I have administrator access to the database. I don't look at anyone's data, and I'd only ever touch it if you asked me to help with a problem in your own account.

03 What never happens

No ads, no analytics, no trackers, no crash reporters. Your data is never sold, shared, rented, or used for anything except showing it back to you.

The only emails you'll get are the ones you trigger: confirming your address and resetting your password.

04 On your iPhone

Your browser keeps a sign-in token so you don't have to log in every time. Signing out removes it. Nothing else about you is stored on the phone.

05 Taking it with you, or deleting it

Settings → Export gives you your entries as CSV, Excel or PDF, and Back up everything gives you the whole book as one file.

Settings → Clear all data permanently deletes everything you've entered. To close your account and remove your email too, write to support.vnktsh@proton.me from the address you signed up with, and it will be deleted within 7 days.

06 Age

Because signing up needs an email address, MoneyBook for iPhone is meant for people aged 13 and over.

07 Changes to this policy

If anything here changes, the effective date above changes with it, and anything that affects how your data is used will be announced in the app before it happens.

08 Contact

Questions or requests: support.vnktsh@proton.me, or the contact page. Both reach me directly.

Made to be shared, not sold.
