Privacy Policy

MoneyBook collects no data. None. You install it, you use it — that's the whole relationship.

Effective 3 September 2026

01 We collect nothing

MoneyBook makes no network requests of any kind. There's no account, no server, no analytics, no crash reporting, no ads, and no trackers — not from us, and not from any third party. Everything you enter lives only in a database on your own device.

There is no connection between us and you once the app is installed. We have no way to see your data, identify you, or know the app is even on your phone.

02 On-device security stays on-device

If you set a passcode or enable Face ID / fingerprint unlock, that's handled entirely by your device's own secure storage and operating system. It never leaves your device, and we never have access to it.

03 Suitable for all ages, including children under 13

Since MoneyBook collects no data from anyone, it's suitable for users of any age, including children under 13.

04 Changes to this policy

If this ever changes — it won't without a very good reason — we'll update the effective date above and notify users in the app.

05 Contact us

Questions? Email support.vnktsh@proton.me.
