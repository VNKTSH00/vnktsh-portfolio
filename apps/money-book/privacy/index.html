Privacy Policy

Covers the MoneyBook app (iOS, Android, macOS, Windows) and moneybook.app. Short version: your finances stay on your device unless you turn on cloud sync, there's no analytics or ads, and your data is never sold.

Effective 3 September 2026

01 Offline-first by default

Your transactions, categories, budgets, and attachments are stored locally on your device using an on-device database. We never see this data unless you explicitly turn on Backup & Sync.

02 Data collected in the app

If you never create an account: MoneyBook collects nothing about you. No analytics, no crash reporting, no ads, no trackers of any kind are built into the app.

If you turn on Backup & Sync: we create an account using the email address (and password, or Google/Apple sign-in) you provide, and your financial data is encrypted in transit and stored with our backend provider, Supabase, so it can sync across your devices. You can turn this off and delete your synced data at any time from Settings, or by emailing us (see Contact, below).

Passcode & biometric unlock: if you set an app passcode or enable Face ID / fingerprint unlock, that data is managed entirely by your device's secure storage and operating system. It never leaves your device and we never have access to it.

Exports: if you export your data as CSV, Excel, or PDF, or attach a photo to a transaction, that file is created and shared directly from your device (e.g. via your device's share sheet) — it does not pass through our servers.

03 Data collected on this website

If you purchase MoneyBook Premium, we collect the email address you provide at checkout and generate a license key tied to it, so the app can verify your purchase. Payment is processed entirely by Stripe — we never receive or store your card details.

04 Who we share data with

We share data only with the service providers needed to run MoneyBook: Supabase (optional cloud sync and account authentication) and Stripe (payment processing for Premium purchases). We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

05 Retention & deletion

Local data lives on your device until you delete the app or clear it yourself. Synced data is kept until you disable Backup & Sync or ask us to delete it. To request deletion of your account and any synced data, email support@moneybook.app and we'll remove it within 30 days.

06 Security

Data in transit to Supabase and Stripe is encrypted (TLS). Optional sync data is encrypted at rest by Supabase. Passcodes and biometric credentials are handled by your device's OS-level secure storage and never transmitted anywhere.

07 Children's privacy

MoneyBook is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

08 Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the effective date above and, for significant changes, notify users in the app.

09 Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email support@moneybook.app.
