📁

Budgeting

Plan by category, by month, or by week — and see exactly how close you are to the line.

  • Monthly & weekly budgets per category
  • Real-time progress bars as you spend
  • Over-budget categories flagged instantly
🎨

Themes

MoneyBook follows your OS by default, or lock in light or dark and pick an accent that fits you.

  • Light & dark, switchable anytime
  • Six accent colours, including Ocean
  • Remembers your choice per device

Backup / restore

Your data lives on your device — back it up on your terms and bring it back in one click.

  • One-click local backup file

A closer look

Every feature, grouped by what it helps you do.

Core budgeting

📊

Dashboard overview

A single glance shows total income, expenses, and remaining budget, broken down by category.

📒

Transaction logging

Add transactions in two taps. Tag them with a category, a note, or a photo of the receipt.

📁

Budget planner

MoneyBook warns you when you're close to the limit and flags over-budget categories in red.

Organize & automate

🏷️

Custom categories

Create, rename, and colour-code your own categories to match how you actually think about money.

🔁

Recurring transactions

Set up rent, subscriptions, salary — anything that repeats. Entries get created on schedule.

🔔

Budget alerts

Get notified when spending in a category hits 80% or 100% of the budget — before it's too late.

Import, export & currency

📥

CSV / bank import

Download a statement from your bank and drop the CSV in — the mapper learns your layout.

🌍

Multi-currency

Track expenses in any currency, converted at the day's rate and totalled in your home currency.

📤

Export & reports

Export transactions to CSV or generate a clean PDF summary — great for tax time.
