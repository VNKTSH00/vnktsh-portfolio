I'm Venkatesh, and I build apps the way I'd cook for people I like — carefully, then handed over with nothing attached. No subscription, no ads, no meter running. The only thing I'm hoping to get back is your reaction.

See what I've made Why I give it away

We have turned creation into transaction. Every tool comes with a subscription; every craft comes with a meter.

But the best things in life are born from a different impulse: hospitality.

Think of a home-cooked meal. You don't measure its worth by the price per plate, but by the reaction of those who share it with you. Software, at its finest, should feel like a feast prepared for friends.

I build these apps open and free because the joy of creation lies in the reaction of the guest, not the size of the tip.

— Venkatesh

House rules

Four things that are true of everything I put on this table.

Nothing costs anything

Free to download, free to keep, free later too. No subscription, no ads, no upgrade nagging at you from the corner of the screen.

One dish, done properly

Every app solves one problem well instead of ten badly. Fewer features, finished ones — nothing half-cooked shipped to fill a roadmap.

Your data stays yours

No accounts, no trackers, no analytics quietly taking notes. MoneyBook keeps everything on your device — there's no server to send it to.

The door stays open

Real versioned updates, not an abandoned side project. If something's broken or missing, tell me — being told is half the reason I do this.

AI is my sous-chef. It chops the vegetables and preps the kitchen — but the recipe, the flavour, and the final dish are entirely mine.

What it costs

Nothing. Not now, not after a trial runs out, not once enough people are using it. There's no business model here waiting to switch on — I'm cooking for whoever shows up, and you don't hand a friend an invoice at the end of dinner.

  • No subscriptions. You don't rent something that was given to you.
  • No ads. Nobody's attention gets sold to cover the cost of the meal.
  • Nothing collected. No accounts, no telemetry, no quiet second copy of your data.
